TETHER

A five-lane rhythm prototype. Your paddle catches. Snap through gaps. Stretch your soul where the paddle can't go.

This demo is played with a keyboard: arrows, Shift and Ctrl or Space.

CONTROLS

←→
Move one lane. Press the other way mid-move to turn back; tap fast to hover between lanes.
Shift + ←→
Snap: teleport one lane. It crosses zips and slips through gaps a move can't.
Ctrl / Space + ←→
Soul: it stretches out on an elastic tether and catches any note, straight through blocks and walls. The farther it goes, the slower it pulls. Let go and it springs back past the paddle. The paddle stays put meanwhile.
Esc R
Pause · restart

A and D work as arrows. On a Mac, Ctrl + arrows switches desktops, so use Space for the soul.

WHAT FALLS
